Factions

Every faction brings its own way of winning: some hold the line, others break it. House Bolton, The Horde, Gondor, Isengard, Númenor and the Army of the Dead are playable end to end; Lannister exists as project material and does not have finished profiles yet.

House Bolton

Playable

“Our Blades Are Sharp”

Heavy infantry that gives no ground and cavalry that punishes anyone out of position. The Dark North Spears absorb the charge with Unbreakable Formation while the Lancers work round the flank. This is a positional faction: you choose where the fight happens and force your opponent to come to you.

The Horde

Playable

Pressure through numbers and through fear. Grunts cost 10 points and fill the table; the Champion causes Terror and punishes enemy Courage within 6″ with Harbinger of Evil, so your opponent starts failing tests before they lose models. Raptor Riders at 10″ close down the flanks and the Trolls take more punishment than their profile suggests.

Gondor

Playable

The line that does not break. Citadel Guard and Warriors of Minas Tirith hold the front with Shieldwall while heroes such as Boromir or Faramir lead from inside the formation. Gandalf can join the same Warband as your Gondor warriors. Two sides of the same faction: Aragorn at 225 points, or a cheap line of Warriors of Minas Tirith at 8.

Isengard

Playable

Disciplined Uruk-hai rather than a disorderly horde. Uruk-hai Warriors form a Shieldwall with pike or crossbow, and heroes such as Lurtz or Uglúk push from the front. Saruman brings the only guaranteed Priority control in the game with the Palantír. Includes the Dunland allies: the Wild Man Oathmaker and his people, fanatical and cheap.

Númenor

Playable

A Gondor sub-list built around Elendil and Isildur: few profiles, but among the toughest in the game — Elendil fights at Fight 8 and Strength 5. If Elendil falls, Isildur can take up the Shards of Narsil over his body. It is played separately from Gondor because the book treats it as its own list.

The Army of the Dead

Playable

The spirits of Dunharrow: high Defence, Terror, and the King of the Dead's Drain Soul rule, which slays instead of wounding. A small sub-list (4 profiles) meant to be combined with Gondor in a game — not to hold up a full army on its own.
