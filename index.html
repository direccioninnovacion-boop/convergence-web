What Convergence is

Convergence is a turn-based miniatures wargame played on a table with terrain. Each player fields an army built to an agreed points limit, split into warbands: a hero in command and the warriors who follow them.

The system is the Middle-earth Strategy Battle Game (Rules Manual 2024). Each turn you roll a dice for Priority, and from there you Move, Shoot and resolve Combats. Heroes spend Might, Will and Fate to bend the dice at the right moment: that is the currency that decides games.

You do not need to buy anything to start trying it out: the army builder puts together legal lists, the profile list carries every statistic, and the game tracker keeps count of casualties and resources for you.

The turn sequence

Five phases, always in the same order. Tap any of them for the summary.

01 Priority +
Both players roll a D6. Whoever rolls higher gains Priority and moves first for the whole turn. On a tie, Priority passes to the player who did not have it the previous turn. It is the roll that decides who charges and who receives: almost the whole turn is played from here.
02 Move +
The player with Priority moves all their models, then their opponent does the same. Each model moves up to its Move Value. Moving into contact with an enemy is a Charge and Engages both models in Combat. Movement Heroic Actions, such as Heroic March, are declared here too.
03 Shoot +
The player with Priority shoots first. You need Line of Sight and the target cannot be Engaged in Combat. Roll against the shooter's Shoot Value, then roll To Wound against the target's Defence. Models with bows are limited to one third of your warriors.
04 Fight +
All Engaged Combats are resolved, starting with the one chosen by the player with Priority. Each side makes its Duel Roll; the winner makes Strikes. Then you roll To Wound, cross-referencing Strength against Defence. Heroes can spend Might to modify the Duel Roll and to declare Heroic Actions such as Heroic Strike.
05 End +
End-of-turn effects and the state of the army are checked. When a side drops below 50 % of its starting models it is Broken: from then on every model that wishes to Move must pass a Courage Test. The game may end when it drops below 25 %.
